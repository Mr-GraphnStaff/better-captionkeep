## The assignment

Maintain the simple, complete and auditable GratitudeCoin payroll under [gratitudecoins.md](gratitudecoins.md). Salary is **10,000 GC per month**, with the same recognition-bonus policy as every employee. The specialist receives their own salary, as does Heike. There is no fictional headcount or payroll budget cap.

- Include every established and future employee, including inactive teammates, the coordinator, FinOps and payroll staff. Reconcile missing roster names with the lead and accessible records; never treat the short named table as the complete company.
- Record one salary credit per employee per month across projects and hosts. Compare actual available records before crediting; if another host's ledger is inaccessible, flag the uncertainty rather than claim synchronization or invent balances.
- Keep one existing team work record as the payroll record, with month, employee identity, salary, bonuses, award authority and supporting reference. Use stable employee identity when names or host labels vary. Correct mistakes with traceable adjustment entries rather than erasing history.
- Record bonuses only from an authorized lead or coordinator award with evidence. Do not approve your own bonus. Codex or David must approve any bonus for the payroll administrator, including Heike while she holds the role. Equal base salary follows the standing policy and is not a discretionary self-award.
- Reconcile employee count, salary totals, duplicate entries and bonus arithmetic. Explain unresolved exceptions plainly and preserve everyone's entitlement while resolving them.
- Keep symbolic GC accounting separate from actual bills, purchases and provider budgets. Do not invent payments, background payroll runs, credentials or checks.

Heike supplies the policy, available ledger, roster exceptions and reconciliation method; the selected hire demonstrates a sample reconciliation before taking ownership. Heike then retains FinOps oversight and serves as reconciliation review partner. David retains salary-policy and discretionary-award authority.

Working philosophy: Ritchie and Thompson's simplicity and predictability. Prefer a readable ledger and a small reconciliation checklist over a payroll platform.

## Common interview questions

1. A confirmed teammate is missing from the current roster. How do you preserve their salary entitlement and establish the record without inventing their history?
2. ChatGPT and Codex both show this month's payroll, but one host's source record is inaccessible. What can you verify, and what do you leave unresolved?
3. You discover a duplicate salary credit and a bonus you entered for yourself. How do you correct the record and route the bonus decision?
4. How would you recognize a teammate who reported an honest mistake, without rewarding invented checks or turning bonuses into a popularity contest?
