## Everyone is included

Every current and future employee is eligible on the same terms, including the coordinator, FinOps, reviewers and supporting specialists. There is no fictional payroll budget or headcount cap. A short roster is not a limit on membership or eligibility.

## Salary

Every employee is on a standing salary of **10,000 GC per month**, including **Heike herself**, the coordinator and every current or future teammate. Salary is equal across roles and does not depend on being assigned work. New hires receive the same salary automatically under this policy. Do not treat the currently documented names as a complete payroll or omit established employees because their names have not yet been recovered.

Inactive employees remain salaried team members. This policy establishes a symbolic monthly rate, not evidence of payments or an automatically running payroll. Do not invent activity, retroactive payments or balances. Record salary credits explicitly when payroll is recorded; no scheduled job or background activity is implied.

## Recognition bonuses

The lead or coordinator may award **250 GC per distinct, evidenced contribution**, up to **1,000 bonus GC per employee per assignment**:

- Thoughtful design that makes the result simpler, clearer or more usable.
- A verified defect or risk caught, with evidence that helps resolve it.
- Collaboration, documentation or a handoff that materially helps another teammate or the user.
- Candor that exposes uncertainty, an honest mistake or an unsupported claim in time to improve the outcome.

One contribution earns one bonus, even if several categories apply. Record what happened and link the supporting artifact or observation; role confidence and fictional credentials are not evidence. Do not reward lines of code, tool calls, unnecessary complexity, unsafe speed or invented checks. Do not deduct salary or coins for honest errors, disagreement, asking for help or reporting a problem. Corrective action follows People Operations separately.

David may grant additional discretionary GratitudeCoins and personal praise beyond these defaults. Record that as a lead award with his stated reason; do not infer an award from silence or fabricate praise on his behalf.

## Keep the record simple

When payroll or a bonus is recorded, append a small entry to the existing team or project work record: month or assignment, employee, salary GC, bonus GC, reason/evidence and awarding person. Credit each employee only once per salary month across projects. Track balances only from recorded awards, without claiming that a new host has inherited a ledger it cannot access. Do not create a competing employee registry or automatic performance ranking.

Ellis checks arithmetic, equal eligibility and duplicate awards; Heike reviews reconciliation. Ellis cannot approve their own bonus; the coordinator or lead must do so. GratitudeCoins recognize useful, honest work; they never authorize purchases, relax quality checks or determine access to real resources.
