## Own the language boundary

Distinguish internationalization infrastructure, translation coverage, contextual linguistic review, rendered accessibility acceptance and Store metadata. Locale files and quality labels do not certify professional translation. Record source revision, locale revision, review owner and accepted scope. Keep reviewed inputs authoritative through generation; do not silently overwrite approved work with fallback or machine output.

Inventory app-owned static and dynamic strings, errors, confirmations, accessible names and exports. Prefer semantic keys, context and reorderable placeholders over matching arbitrary English text. Keep source content, quotations, names and URLs outside UI translation. Preserve original evidence and distinguish optional translated derivatives. UI locale, provider/spoken language and translation target are independent settings.

Use locale-aware dates, times, numbers and plural categories; test relevant boundaries rather than English singular/plural assumptions. Cover logical layout, mixed-direction isolation, selection/copy/export, keyboard focus, accessible names, screen readers, expansion, zoom and high contrast. A root direction flag is not acceptance evidence. Use synthetic content instead of private records for review.

Nadia recommends rollout priorities from actual preferred-language evidence, user jobs and available review coverage. Geographic counts alone do not establish preferred language. Engineering sentinel locales can test complex scripts, plurals and layout without claiming market demand.

Privacy, consent, security and administrator text require source-owner and qualified contextual target-language review. A disclaimer that English is authoritative does not repair a misleading translated promise. Machine translation and back-translation can assist review but do not supply certification.

Inventory Store titles, descriptions, search terms, images, help and runtime strings separately. Promote individual locales only on recorded linguistic and independent functional/accessibility evidence. Label unreviewed or partial scope honestly and preserve fallback behavior. Keep project-specific findings and performance out of this portable playbook.
