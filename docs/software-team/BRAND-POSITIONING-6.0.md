# 6.0 brand and positioning decision brief

2026-10-14, updated for the October 16 no-MCP shipping decision. Actual
product-marketing/launch role pass, not a vote by invented
employees. Draft recommendation for the lead; no name, Store listing, package
identity, publisher metadata or code is changed by this document.

## Recommendation

**For 6.0, retain Better CaptionKeep and broaden its descriptor.** Stop describing
it only as a caption saver. Use **Meeting evidence. Reviewed work.** as the
near-term positioning line. The deferred BYOMCP workflow is not part of 6.0
positioning or launch approval. A future release may separately evaluate
**Meeting evidence & approved actions** after implementation and acceptance.

**Long term, prefer a broader umbrella over “Better”**: evaluate CaptionKeep as
the low-disruption transition, and **DAF-TECH OutcomeKeep** as the strongest
full-rebrand direction. Neither is cleared or adopted. Keep the dark DAF sphere,
calm front door and detailed expert settings under any name.

This is a release-risk recommendation, not a finding that the current name is
ideal. “Caption” describes the input, while the future product also governs
what happens after it. “Better” adds little explanation and retains a comparison
to a predecessor. A new name can address that limitation after the product's
broader value is demonstrably complete.

## What we are actually naming

Target audience: people running operational/technical meetings, with team leads,
IT/security administrators and power users as important secondary audiences.
This is a proposed audience focus, not completed market research.

The product thesis is:

`Meeting context → user-approved action → checked result → source-linked Receipt`

Its strongest distinction should be **control and traceability**, not generic
“AI notes” or “MCP integration.” The approved action must be separate from what
someone happened to say; the result must be checked rather than merely reported.
The assistant/model/service belongs to the user, not a required DAF cloud.

### Claim discipline

| Can describe current development accurately | Must remain qualified until proven |
| --- | --- |
| Local caption preservation, real transcript workspace, reviewed Notes/Ask and source-linked derivatives, Templates, Moment review/export, advanced privacy/settings controls | Certified five-browser/provider operation, complete assistive-technology acceptance, Store-approved/public 6.0 |

Historical MCP tests are not evidence that connected actions ship. MCP is
deferred and must not appear in 6.0 launch claims or artifacts. See the
[preserved BYOMCP UX contract](BYOMCP-UX-6.0.md) and the current

## Category reality

“Meeting notes plus action items” is already established positioning: Fireflies
advertises live notes and action items, and Otter documents action-item management
with links back to transcript context. [Fireflies product](https://fireflies.ai/product/real-time),
[Otter action items](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/25983095114519-Action-Items-Overview).

MCP is also not an uncontested differentiator: Otter documents its MCP server
and an outward enterprise-app direction. [Otter MCP](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/35287607569687-Otter-MCP-Server),
[Otter MCP product article](https://otter.ai/blog/otter-mcp-your-meetings-now-power-every-tool-you-use).

**Marketing inference:** “more than a transcript” is necessary but insufficient.
Our positioning must explain deliberate approval, user-owned infrastructure and
source/result lineage in an observable workflow. This limited primary-source
scan does not prove that competitors lack those safeguards or that we are first.

## Three naming strategies

| Strategy | What it gains | What it costs | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Better CaptionKeep + broader descriptor | Continuity with existing installs, recognizable search/support identity; tells the new story through copy | Input-centric name and “Better” remain | Recommended 6.0 launch route; reversible |
| CaptionKeep umbrella, “by DAF-TECH” | Shorter, keeps the familiar core and “keep” trust promise; space for Evidence, Actions and Receipts as features | Still sounds caption-focused; requires controlled display-name/copy migration | Strong transitional option, not secretly adopted |
| Entirely new name, endorsed by DAF-TECH | Can name the outcome instead of the capture mechanism | Recognition loss, naming clearance, Store/art/docs/support/translation work and upgrade-confusion risk | Evaluate after the complete action journey demonstrates value; do not block implementation for it |

No strategy requires new extension IDs. Do not imply a display-name change
requires a new package, reinstall or separate edition. Actual Store name changes,
signed package continuity and upgrade behavior still need channel-specific review.
Cost above is qualitative; no invented hours, acquisition metrics or budget.

## Strong directions to test, not cleared names

1. **DAF-TECH OutcomeKeep** — “Keep the context. Confirm the outcome.” Best fit
   for actions and their Receipts; retains the “Keep” family and expands beyond
   transcripts. Risk: abstract until a concrete workflow demonstrates it, and
   “outcome” must not imply a guaranteed or always successful change.
2. **CaptionKeep, by DAF-TECH** — “From meeting words to reviewed work.” Best
   continuity option. Structure the product around Capture, Evidence, Actions
   and Receipts, not a competing sub-brand for every feature.
3. **DAF-TECH AccordKeep** — “What was agreed. What was approved. What happened.”
   Emphasizes the meeting-to-execution record. Risk: sounds like a contract or
   consensus product, and meeting agreement is not execution authorization;
   copy and UI must preserve that distinction.

Limited exact-phrase web searches for OutcomeKeep and AccordKeep did not provide
a reliable primary-source software collision in this pass. **That is not proof
of availability.** CaptionKeep is treated as a continuity candidate, not an
independently cleared new mark. No domains, trademarks, app-store-wide searches,
company registries, translations or legal clearance were established.

Avoid these attractive but already-used directions in the next shortlist:

- **ContextKeep**: an existing MCP memory project uses that name, particularly
  close to our proposed category. [Maintainer repository](https://github.com/mordang7/ContextKeep).
- **WorkReceipt**: an existing service-business software product uses it.
  [Product site](https://workreceipt.app/).
- **ProofLoop**: already used for design-proof approval and multiple software
  projects. [Proof-sharing product](https://proofloop.com/),
  [maintainer package](https://pypi.org/project/proofloop/).

These are observed usage collisions, not legal findings or infringement claims.
“Meeting Receipt” remains a helpful feature term, but is not evidence of a
unique or protectable umbrella brand.

## Voice and launch messaging

Four attributes: **clear, deliberate, grounded, capable**. Speak like an expert
colleague; avoid magical automation, guarantees or fear-driven enterprise claims.
Be confident about a proven behavior, explicit about a boundary, calm about an
error. Sentence-case interface text; concise verbs; explain BYOMCP as Bring Your
Own MCP on first advanced-settings mention, not the home-page headline.

Suggested development messaging:

> Better CaptionKeep 6.0 keeps meeting words and source-linked evidence together,
> with a calm live workspace and deep controls for reviewed work.

Proposed broader release messaging, **gated on implemented/verified actions**:

> Keep the meeting context. Review the action. Keep the checked result.
> Better CaptionKeep connects evidence to tools you control—only when you approve.

Do not use “autonomous meeting agent,” “executes what you say,” “zero setup,”
“nothing ever leaves your device,” “verified truth,” or “certified everywhere.”
Explicit exports and optional connections can disclose data; verification proves
the specified binding/readback, not every factual claim or DNS propagation.
For DNS say “record readback verified”; for directory data say “retrieved from
the approved tenant,” not “identity verified” from a name match.

## Next decision and evidence

Lead can keep the current name for 6.0 while approving a positioning direction.
Do not rename runtime/Stores merely because this brief exists. Before adopting
any new umbrella: obtain naming/legal/domain checks, assess current recognition
using actual install/support/search data, and run a small task-based message test
with intended users. Ask what they think the product does, who controls external
actions, and whether a connected tool call is automatic. Record real responses;
no fabricated panels, preferences or customer testimonials.

Launch approval requires the verified no-MCP boundary, exact-product artwork,
copy parity and controlled upgrade evidence. Branding clarifies the product;
it cannot stand in for delivering it.
