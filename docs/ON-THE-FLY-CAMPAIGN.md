# On the Fly campaign

## Creative spine

**If your meeting notes can't show their work, fuck 'em.**

That is the uncensored DAF-TECH owner-channel line. It is intentionally not
for the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons, enterprise app catalogs, or
paid placements with profanity restrictions.

Marketplace-safe campaign line:

**If your meeting notes can't show their work, don't trust them.**

Product line:

**Select it. Research it. Keep the evidence.**

## Product truth beneath the attitude

- Highlight meeting words or choose a full caption.
- Choose Research, Explain, Draft a live reply, or Draft a follow-up email.
- Review the exact source-linked prompt inside Better CaptionKeep.
- Explicitly copy and open the user's chosen AI workspace.
- Confirm the workspace, paste, review, and send there.
- No publisher AI account, Azure MCP, extension API key, automatic paste,
  automatic send, or fabricated return channel.

## Visual system

The On the Fly orb is the kinetic feature mark. The DAF-TECH sphere is the
maker seal. Black glass, emerald, cyan, evidence trails, and hard white type
create the campaign world. Store screenshots use deterministic synthetic
meeting content; cinematic campaign artwork never masquerades as product UI.
