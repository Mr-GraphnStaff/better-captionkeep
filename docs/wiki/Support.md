# Support and Bug Reports

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Mr-GraphnStaff/better-captionkeep/issues) for reproducible product defects and feature requests.

## Include

- Browser name and version.
- Better CaptionKeep version from the extension card.
- Meeting provider: Teams, Google Meet, or Zoom Web.
- Store installation or the exact unpacked lane.
- What you expected, what happened, and reproducible steps.
- Sanitized screenshots of the extension interface or browser error.

## Never include

- Transcript content or participant names.
- Meeting join links or meeting IDs.
- Tenant identifiers unless privately requested by an authorized administrator.
- Passwords, MFA codes, access or refresh tokens, cookies, client secrets, or private keys.

For security-sensitive reports, follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Mr-GraphnStaff/better-captionkeep/blob/master/SECURITY.md) instead of posting exploitable details publicly.
