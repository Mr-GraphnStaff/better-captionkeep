# Better CaptionKeep 5.4 Store artwork

These assets use synthetic meeting content and the current 5.4 interaction
model. They contain no real participant, tenant, meeting, transcript, token, or
customer data.

The five screenshots form one product story: three supported meeting platforms,
explicit source controls, On the Fly Research, cited live-chat preparation, and
customer-controlled enterprise boundaries. Scribble remains the Better
CaptionKeep icon. The sphere and the line **A DAF-TECH Product** identify the
parent brand.

`source.html` is the deterministic artwork source. Render screenshots at
1280 x 800 with `?slide=1` through `?slide=5`, the small tile at 440 x 280 with
`?promo=small`, and the large tile at 1400 x 560 with `?promo=large`.
