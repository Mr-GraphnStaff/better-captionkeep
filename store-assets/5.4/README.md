# Better CaptionKeep 5.4 Store artwork

These assets use synthetic meeting content and the current 5.4 interaction
model. They contain no real participant, tenant, meeting, transcript, token, or
customer data.

The retained screenshots cover verified product behavior: three supported
meeting platforms, explicit source controls, and customer-controlled enterprise
boundaries. Screenshots that depicted automatic assistant research or cited
live-chat preparation were removed when that experiment left the 5.4 scope.
Scribble remains the Better CaptionKeep icon. The sphere and the line
**A DAF-TECH Product** identify the parent brand.

`source.html` remains the deterministic artwork source while the replacement
five-image Store story is redesigned. Do not publish slides 3 or 4 from the
current source. Render the small tile at 440 x 280 with `?promo=small`, and the
large tile at 1400 x 560 with `?promo=large`.
